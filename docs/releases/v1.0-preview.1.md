Mic Pause is a native macOS menu-bar utility that pauses Apple Music when your microphone is active and resumes when you’re done.

## Included in this preview

- A refreshed menu-bar dashboard and a dedicated Settings window.
- Configurable automatic resume with immediate, 1-, 2-, or 5-second delays.
- System-wide capture-source detection, including non-default microphone use, with per-source ignore controls.
- Known background listeners ignored by default; Dictation, Siri, and capture helpers remain eligible unless explicitly ignored.
- Launch at Login with a persistent opt-out and registration repair after the app moves.
- Shared app and menu-bar artwork, current screenshots, and build/release documentation.

## Install

Requires **macOS 14 or later**, Apple silicon or Intel, and the Apple Music app.

Download `Mic-Pause-1.0-preview.1-universal.dmg`, open it, drag **Mic Pause** into **Applications**, and eject the disk image. Open the app and allow Apple Music Automation when prompted. Mic Pause appears in the menu bar and enables Launch at Login on first launch; this can be turned off in Settings.

**Signing status:** the app is Apple Development signed. This preview is **not notarized by Apple**, and the disk image itself is unsigned. Gatekeeper may block the download. If you trust it, review **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after trying to open the installed app.

The accompanying `.sha256` file verifies the DMG download; the `.INSTALL.txt` repeats the installation and signing information.

## Privacy and limits

The app reads microphone activity metadata; it does not capture audio, record conversations, collect analytics, or make network requests. It controls Apple Music only. Detection depends on macOS and audio drivers; a process that holds its input open can keep music paused.

Report issues with the macOS version, microphone, and reproduction steps at https://github.com/dp466/MusicMicPause/issues.
